# Research brief

- **Working title:** JEPA world models for finance: learning predictable latent state and dynamics from partially observed financial data
- **Research question(s):** Can a joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) be trained as a world model of a financial system, so that its latent state is useful and its latent dynamics are credible, given scarce, noisy, non-stationary and partially observed data? Four sub-questions (sharpened after the review):
  1. **Representation:** how to encode the current state (assets, firms, macro, text) so that context→target prediction in embedding space captures predictable structure rather than noise. Is tabular/time-series enough, or does multi-modality (filings, news, graphs) pay for itself?
  2. **Partial observability:** how to infer latent state from incomplete, irregular, mixed-frequency observations (e.g. private-firm fundamentals, delayed disclosures).
  3. **Dynamics:** how to learn (action-conditioned) latent transitions; what can and cannot be identified from mostly observational data with rare, confounded "actions" (rate decisions, shocks).
  4. **Use:** which downstream finance uses the world model actually enables (scenario analysis, nowcasting/interpolation, counterfactual "what-if", network shock propagation), and how to evaluate each honestly.
- **Field / sub-area:** AI in finance; self-supervised / representation learning; world models; financial time series; causal inference (for counterfactual uses).
- **PI expertise & angle:** Industry practitioner and academic in AI in finance. PI's prior candidate uses: (a) scenario generation conditioned on events/actions (Fed rate, war, COVID); (b) firm fundamentals (revenue, headcount, cash flow) incl. counterfactual what-ifs, limited by disclosure; (c) supply chain. PI's framing: start narrow; problem definition is the hard part.
- **Goal & target venue / deadline:** Finance-AI venue (e.g. ICAIF, KDD applied/finance tracks). No fixed deadline yet.
- **Scope in:** JEPA-family and related latent-predictive world models (I-JEPA, V-JEPA/V-JEPA 2(-AC), time-series/tabular/graph JEPAs, other latent world models used as baselines/comparators); financial foundation models and time-series foundation models as baselines; data-efficient pretraining; evaluation of world models (forecasting, nowcasting, counterfactual/scenario validity); modality choice decided by the review.
- **Scope out (initially):** Trading-strategy alpha claims as the headline result (allowed only as one downstream probe with proper backtest hygiene); LLM-only "world model" prompting without a trained predictive model; large-scale video/robotics JEPA work except as methodological source.
- **Resources:** Compute **undetermined**: PI wants a budget estimate after the review (checkpoint before any GPU spend). Data: free public (FRED, EDGAR/XBRL, prices, BLS, GDELT), academic subscriptions (WRDS: CRSP/Compustat; Refinitiv/Bloomberg via university), alt data (job postings, supply-chain graphs, etc.), and proprietary RockAI/employer data subject to approval.
- **Modality plan (PI, 2026-10-01):** start **tabular / time-series**. Multi-modal is a planned later extension: SEC filings and internal company documents (text, tables, figures) as additional context/target views. The review should cover document/filing representation learning enough to scope that extension.
- **Preferences:** Empirical; start from a narrow wedge chosen after the review; credible to both ML and finance audiences. Wedge (macro scenarios / firm nowcasting / supply chain / other) to be **decided by the review**.
- **Ethics / data constraints:** Proprietary data never leaves the machine and is never the paper's only basis; results must be reproducible on public or academically licensed data. Respect WRDS/vendor licence terms (no redistribution of raw data). Strict point-in-time data and leakage controls (no look-ahead, survivorship bias, restatement leakage).
- **Success looks like:** A clearly defined finance world-modelling problem with a JEPA approach that beats strong baselines (time-series foundation models, generative/reconstructive SSL, classical econometric models) on a pre-registered held-out evaluation, plus an honest account of what the latent dynamics can and cannot support (especially counterfactual claims).

## Assistant notes on framing (2026-10-01, to be tested in the review)
- JEPA is non-generative: it predicts target *embeddings* from context embeddings and does not model the joint distribution or reconstruct inputs. Scenario *generation* therefore needs an extra component (decoder, generative head, or latent sampling + probe readout).
- Multi-modality is optional: context/target can be different fields, entities or time windows of one tabular panel.
- Predictable ≠ causal. Counterfactual "what-if" needs action-conditioning plus an identification argument; financial "actions" are rare, endogenous and confounded. Treat causal use as the highest-risk sub-question.
