## 0. Bottom line (for the PI)
1. **No verified "financial world model" exists in industry or in the arXiv record as of 2026-10-01** (M-H). Title search "financial world model" on arXiv since 2025-10-01 returned 25 hits, none a latent predictive world model of markets or firms (they are LLM-agent benchmarks, generators or surveys); web searches for banks/funds/start-ups launching one found only physical-AI/robotics "world model" companies (e.g. a news item on 23 new Chinese world-model start-ups in 7 months of 2026 [2nd, S], all 4D/embodied). The only finance-adjacent products are time-series foundation models (TSFMs) that forecast values, not latent-state/action-conditioned models. So the wedge is open, but absence of evidence from marketing-light finance firms is weak evidence (they do not publish).
2. **JEPA's industrial momentum is in physical AI, not finance.** Meta released V-JEPA 2 (code + weights, commercial use allowed); LeCun left Meta in 2025 and founded AMI Labs (JEPA-based, $1.03B seed, Mar 2026) with stated targets of industrial/robotics/healthcare, no finance vertical. Expect JEPA tooling and recipes (LeWM, SIGReg) to come from robotics, to be adapted by us.
3. **Finance-specific TSFMs now exist in 2026 and are the real baselines**: Kronos (AAAI 2026), FinCast, EXAONE Finance 1.0 (LG AI Research, synthetic-only pretraining), plus general TSFMs (Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, Moirai 2.0, TimeGPT). Central banks are building macro TSFMs (BIS BISTRO, 2026). Independent evidence that any of these beat simple benchmarks on returns is weak (see eval-data partial).
4. **Regulators changed the model-risk rulebook in April 2026** (SR 26-2 replacing SR 11-7) and, per one trade-press report, left generative/agentic AI out of scope. A new class of latent world model used for scenario analysis would sit in a governance gap; for a bank-facing paper, document validation (backtests, sensitivity, outcomes analysis) in SR 11-7 terms anyway.

## 6. Industry vs academia: what differs
- **Evaluation realism.** Central-bank and regulatory work uses real-time vintages and fixed baselines (DFM/BVAR/AR); the academic TSFM literature mostly does not (compare MACROCAST `arXiv:2606.28670`, which does, with `arXiv:2506.15705`, which back-tests TSFMs on a univariate series). Industry publishes little quantitative evidence (Two Sigma, JPM pages: none).
- **Data.** Banks and funds have proprietary, point-in-time, entity-level data (transactions, positions); academics rely on FRED/CRSP/Compustat/EDGAR, whose licences and as-reported timing differ (see eval-data partial dataset table).
- **Claims.** Vendor TSFM claims (EXAONE, FinCast, Kronos) are self-benchmarked; independent tests find small gains (`arXiv:2606.27100`). Treat vendor numbers as marketing until replicated.
- **Governance.** Model-risk regime (SR 26-2) targets models used in decisions; a world model used for scenario analysis would need documented conceptual soundness and outcomes analysis. Gen-AI scope gap noted above.
