## 1. JEPA family foundations
**Taxonomy.** (a) Image/video JEPAs with EMA targets and masking: I-JEPA arXiv:2301.08243, V-JEPA arXiv:2404.08471, V-JEPA 2 arXiv:2506.09985, MC-JEPA arXiv:2307.12698 [A for all]. (b) Collapse-control families: variance-covariance regularisation (VICReg arXiv:2105.04906), self-distillation with EMA (BYOL arXiv:2006.07733), redundancy reduction (Barlow Twins arXiv:2103.03230), and the new teacher-free SIGReg (LeJEPA arXiv:2511.08544 [FT]). (c) Probabilistic/variational JEPAs: VJEPA arXiv:2601.14354 [FT], Var-JEPA arXiv:2603.20111 [A].
**What changed in the last 12 months (M).** The field moved from EMA/stop-gradient heuristics to *explicit anti-collapse regularisers with one hyper-parameter*: LeJEPA (SIGReg, lambda about 0.05, 1024 slices, no teacher; arXiv:2511.08544) and its first world-model use LeWorldModel arXiv:2603.19312 [A: "only two loss terms", about 15M parameters, one GPU, "up to 48x faster planning" than foundation-model world models, per abstract]. Time-series JEPAs adopted SIGReg (HEPA arXiv:2605.11130 [FT], Phys-JEPA arXiv:2606.16076 [FT], the benchmark arXiv:2609.31680 [FT]); the tabular recipe paper still needed EMA and still saw collapse (arXiv:2609.25541 [A]).
**Scale of evidence.** V-JEPA 2 pretrains on over 1M hours of video and post-trains the action-conditioned V-JEPA 2-AC on under 62 hours of "unlabeled" Droid robot video (wording per abstract, arXiv:2506.09985; the AC model is action-conditioned, so I read "unlabeled" as no task or reward labels). That is the existence proof that a *small amount of action-labelled data on top of a large passive pretrain* suffices for planning; it is robotics with controllable actions, and finance has no 1M-hour passive corpus of comparable homogeneity (H on the fact, M on the analogy).
**Theory: what JEPA learns.**
- Implicit bias to influential features: in deep linear models JEPA prefers features with high regression coefficients, which filters noisy low-influence features that reconstruction captures (arXiv:2407.03475 [A]). Caveat: linear setting only. Earlier empirical counterpart: JEPA matches or beats reconstruction when distractor noise changes every step but fails when the noise is *fixed* (arXiv:2211.10831 [A]: "slow features"). Slowly varying nuisance components (e.g. persistent macro level, firm fixed effects, trends) are therefore the failure mode to expect in finance (M, speculation on transfer).
- Koopman view: idealised JEPA minimisers are regime indicator functions (Koopman eigenfunctions with eigenvalue 1) and the linear predictor must be near identity (arXiv:2511.09783 [FT]); synthetic purity 65.5% vs 38.8% for an autoencoder, 18 regimes, 180,000 pairs. Supports "JEPA encodes regimes", says little about within-regime variation (M).
- Generalisation: JEPA risk = low-rank factorisation of an action-conditioned co-occurrence matrix; planning regret bounded by sqrt of JEPA risk; approximation vs sample error trade-off in latent dimension k (arXiv:2606.27014 [FT]). Needs deterministic dynamics for the multi-step bound (M that this limits finance use).
- Causal identifiability: component-wise identifiability up to invertible maps and permutation *if* actions induce sufficiently diverse changes in latent transition mechanisms and the observation map is invertible (arXiv:2609.31161 [FT]); degrades with few actions or non-invertible observation. Finance violates both in the obvious way (rare, endogenous actions; many-to-one observations) (H on the conditions, M on the "violates" inference because the confounded-action case is not covered by the extracted theorem).
- Probabilistic view: JEPA as deterministic specialisation of a latent-variable model with explicit ELBO (arXiv:2603.20111 [A]); VJEPA claims representations are sufficient information states for control and links to predictive-state representations and Bayesian filtering, with a collapse-avoidance theorem; only a qualitative linear "Noisy TV" experiment, diagonal-Gaussian predictive distributions (arXiv:2601.14354 [FT]) (L-M: theory-heavy, thin evidence, single author).
**Open problems stated by authors.** Mechanism of backbone dependence unknown (arXiv:2609.31680); EMA dynamics not analysed (arXiv:2511.09783); predictive-distribution expressivity and optimisation stability (arXiv:2601.14354).

**Synthesis (M).** (1) Positive reports (TS-JEPA, CF-JEPA, HEPA, Distributed JEPA, T-JEPA) are mostly on small standard benchmarks, per-dataset pretraining, self-chosen baselines, and none evaluate on non-stationary financial panels. (2) Two independent, larger, more adversarial evaluations are negative or mixed: sign flips by backbone (arXiv:2609.31680) and trailing value-only training on 147 datasets (arXiv:2609.25541 [A]). (3) Collapse is recurrent, and every group fixes it differently ([REG] token, EMA difference, SIGReg, ELBO), so anti-collapse design is a first-order modelling choice. (4) Beating "supervised from scratch with the same epochs" is the right null; only arXiv:2609.31680 uses it cleanly and finds it is not reliably beaten. (5) HEPA is the only item with a financial regime target (VIX) and it is event prediction, not state dynamics.
**Open problems stated by authors.** Ablate predictor vs regulariser separately (arXiv:2609.31680); sensor-localised events and absent precursors (arXiv:2605.11130); dataset-specific physics only partly interpretable (arXiv:2606.16076).

## 3. Latent world models as comparators
**Reconstruction/RL family:** PlaNet/RSSM arXiv:1811.04551 [A], DreamerV3 arXiv:2301.04104 [A], TD-MPC2 arXiv:2310.16828 [A], Ha & Schmidhuber world models arXiv:1803.10122 [A], Genie (latent action video model) arXiv:2402.15391 [A]. **Pre-trained-feature planning:** DINO-WM predicts DINOv2 patch features from offline trajectories and plans zero-shot, no rewards or inverse models (arXiv:2411.04983, per abstract). **JEPA planning:** reward-free offline latent dynamics trained with JEPA plan better on unseen layouts and are more data-efficient than model-free offline RL, equal at trajectory stitching, robust to suboptimal data (arXiv:2502.14819 [A]). **LeWM** arXiv:2603.19312 as above; **PLDM** is the multi-term-loss JEPA baseline named in several papers (not separately retrieved).
**Frontier (2026, mostly robotics/driving):** a dozen action-conditioned JEPA variants are in the library from the early scan (e.g. Delta-JEPA arXiv:2606.31232, Hamiltonian JEPA arXiv:2609.33497, PhyLatent arXiv:2608.05720, Qantara arXiv:2607.04978); diagnostics: JEPA world models are not robust to visual perturbations and an ACPC diagnostic predicts prediction/cost changes across four control tasks (arXiv:2608.12939 [A]); a survey exists (arXiv:2606.00133).
**Latent actions from observational data.** LAPO infers latent actions from action-free demonstrations (arXiv:2312.10812 [A]); LAPA pretrains VLAs from unlabeled videos (arXiv:2410.11758 [A]); object-centric latent actions mitigate action-correlated distractors, cutting distractor harm by 50% (arXiv:2502.09680 [A]); information-bottleneck latent actions maximise mutual information with true actions (arXiv:2508.08743 [A]); latent-action world models with about 10x fewer action labels on DMC (arXiv:2512.10016 [A]); continuous but constrained latent actions work in-the-wild where vector quantisation does not, but they localise relative to the camera because there is no common embodiment (arXiv:2601.05230 [A]); RL from passive data via latent intentions (arXiv:2304.04782 [A]). **Key point for finance (M):** every one of these relies on the observed change being *caused by an agent's action* and on a mapping from latent to real actions learned from a small labelled set. Fed-rate decisions or wars are not low-dimensional, repeatable, exogenous "controls"; the closest analogue is learning a latent "shock" variable and mapping it to labelled events, whose identification would have to be argued, not assumed (arXiv:2609.31161).
**The only direct economics counterfactual test I found:** DSGE-Gym (arXiv:2607.03144 [FT]): learned world models (ridge, MLP, LSTM, Transformer, NextLat) are accurate on-path but collapse off-path (5-sigma tail RMSE up to ~40x the on-path level, per abstract; the WebFetch extraction said 100x and was wrong, corrected 2026-10-01 spot-check); DSGE-generated coverage helps a lot (10-280x on regime shifts) but a first-order DSGE oracle is worse than an MLP off-path; authors warn synthetic data can be confidently wrong if the model is misspecified. No JEPA/Dreamer/TD-MPC2 evaluated, which is a gap (H on the gap within this library).
**Comparator recommendation (M):** DreamerV3/TD-MPC2 are RL-centric (reward-driven), so as *baselines for a no-reward state-prediction problem* they are awkward; stronger and cheaper comparators are (i) a reconstructive latent state-space model (RSSM-style or deep Kalman/DVBF arXiv:1511.05121, arXiv:1605.06432), (ii) a plain next-latent predictor like NextLat in DSGE-Gym, (iii) classical dynamic factor models (arXiv:2007.11887 already in scan), and (iv) AR/HAR/DeepAR (arXiv:2606.03184).

## 4. Partial observability, belief states, SSMs, missingness
- **Belief in latent models:** Wasserstein Belief Updater learns latent POMDP model and belief update with guarantees (arXiv:2303.03284 [A]); Kalman-filter layer with closed-form Gaussian inference as drop-in recurrent layer, parallel scan, wins when uncertainty reasoning matters (arXiv:2409.16824 [A]); world models to handle delayed observations by reducing delayed POMDP to delayed MDP, up to +30% over naive model-based (arXiv:2403.12309 [A]); Neural Bayesian Filtering maintains multimodal beliefs in embedding space, avoids particle impoverishment (arXiv:2510.03614 [A]); SSM+LSTM belief encoder robust to observation dropout, keeps up to 80% of peak performance at 50% availability, but trained with reconstruction (arXiv:2509.11225 [A]); latent safety filters can be myopic if latent lacks safety-relevant features (arXiv:2510.06492 [A], relevant to "what the latent can't see").
- **JEPA-specific:** UWM-JEPA (arXiv:2605.25313 [FT]): density-matrix latent with unitary predictor; teacher-forced JEPA makes actions inert (norm ratio 0.03) while counterfactual targets restore sensitivity; 0.770 vs 0.530 accuracy on a hidden-velocity task, equal probe quality (R^2 0.901 vs 0.894), so the gain is in the predictor; only synthetic oscillator/CartPole; needs simulator state for action binding. VJEPA (arXiv:2601.14354) gives the theoretical Bayesian-filtering link. Learning state-space models from arbitrary observations with JEPA + neural ODEs, pendulum images only (doi:10.48550/arxiv.2508.10489 [A]). (M overall; L on any claim that this works on real noisy data.)
- **Deep SSMs as encoders:** S4 arXiv:2111.00396 [A] and Mamba arXiv:2312.00752 [A] as long-sequence encoders; Mamba-style self-supervised pretraining exists in audio and medical images (not retrieved as records; no JEPA+Mamba time-series paper found, L confidence in absence because arXiv search was partly failing, see Tool notes). SSM layers are the natural fit for irregular/long financial histories, but no evidence yet in JEPA.
- **Missingness/irregular sampling in SSL (pre-2025 foundations):** STraTS treats series as observation triplets with continuous value embedding and forecasting as proxy (doi:10.1145/3516367 [A]); PrimeNet time-sensitive contrastive + reconstruction pre-training for irregular series (AAAI 2023, doi:10.1609/aaai.v37i6.25876 [A]); T-Rep time-embeddings, more resilient to missing data (arXiv:2310.04486 [A]); PLanTS periodicity-aware latent-state SSL with next-transition prediction (arXiv:2509.05478 [A]); encoder-decoder view of irregular series as missing data (arXiv:2008.07599 [A]). **Gap (M):** no paper found that does JEPA-style latent prediction with *irregular, mixed-frequency, informatively missing* data (the finance case: quarterly filings vs daily prices). Distributed JEPA's robustness to missing data (arXiv:2609.17029 [A]) is the nearest.

## 5. Generating from a JEPA (scenario sampling)
JEPA predictors are deterministic point regressors in latent space; scenario generation needs one of:
1. **Variational/probabilistic predictor:** VJEPA (diagonal Gaussian predictive distribution over future latents; credible intervals by sampling; arXiv:2601.14354 [FT]); Var-JEPA ELBO with observation reconstruction (arXiv:2603.20111 [A]); VJ-VCR with latent variables for non-deterministic video (arXiv:2412.10925 [A]).
2. **Finite-support multi-hypothesis:** Branch-JEPA (K=6 latent successors decoded separately, trained on Energy Score; arXiv:2607.05238 [FT]); Energy Score also gives a proper scoring rule for scenario evaluation.
3. **Flow-matching latent dynamics:** Flow-JEPA (arXiv:2608.29029 [FT]) predicts the whole latent trajectory with an 8-step Euler ODE and is more robust to perturbations (67% to 86% success), but is *deterministic at inference* and ~3.8x slower; not a sampler as published (H, from the extraction).
4. **Decoders:** reconstruction decoders defeat JEPA's noise-filtering rationale; Branch-JEPA shows decoders can be added post hoc to latent atoms; for finance a *probe/readout decoder to observables* (returns, spreads, fundamentals) trained on frozen latents is the cheapest design (design suggestion, not evidence).
5. **Alternative:** generative financial models (diffusion/flow/VAEs) already used for scenarios (benchmarked in arXiv:2606.03184: TSFlow, TimeGrad, RATD; scenario generation survey-style comparison arXiv:2606.11859 in scan). A JEPA scenario generator must beat these on proper scoring rules (CRPS/Energy Score) and tail calibration, not on MSE.
**Open problem (M):** no published JEPA has been evaluated for *distributional* fidelity (calibration, tails, cross-sectional dependence) on stochastic real-world time series; Branch-JEPA and VJEPA are the two templates and both are tested on driving/navigation/linear toy systems.

## 6. Data efficiency in small-data regimes
- In-domain low-data SSL pre-training beats generic large-scale transfer for domain-specific downstream tasks (vision comparative study arXiv:2404.17202 [A]; LeJEPA on Galaxy10: 82.72% full / 29.42% 1-shot vs DINOv2 transfer 78.34% / 21.05%, arXiv:2511.08544 [FT]).
- VideoMAE works on 3-4k videos with extreme masking; data quality > quantity (arXiv:2203.12602 [A]) (reconstructive SSL is data-efficient too: JEPA is not uniquely so).
- JEPA time series: HEPA 92% of full-label performance with 2% of labels and under a minute of pretraining per dataset (arXiv:2605.11130 [FT]); label efficiency is the best-supported claim, but datasets are small, pretrain is per-dataset, and baselines include a foundation model not fine-tuned the same way (L-M on fairness).
- Finance-specific counterweight: in FinStressTS neural forecasters need 2-3x more data than AR/HAR for the same calibration and plateau early (arXiv:2606.03184 [FT]); T-JEPA/tabular negative result shows compute cost 1.66x (arXiv:2609.25541 [A]); the generalisation bound's k trade-off says small data wants small latent dimension (arXiv:2606.27014 [FT]).
- V-JEPA 2-AC: under 62 hours of robot video after large passive pretraining (arXiv:2506.09985 [A]) is the template for "few action labels", but only after passive pretraining on 1M hours; the finance analogue would be pretraining on many time series (cross-asset, cross-firm panels) then adapting on few event-labelled episodes (design suggestion).
**Open problem (H):** there is no controlled study of JEPA vs reconstructive SSL vs supervised across *training-set size* on non-stationary financial data. This is a clean, cheap pilot (HEPA-style per-dataset training takes minutes).

## 7. Cross-cutting findings that matter for the PI's four pillars
1. Representation: JEPA is not reliably better than supervised/reconstructive training on tabular/time-series (arXiv:2609.31680, arXiv:2609.25541); the case must be made on tasks where noise filtering matters and with strong simple baselines (arXiv:2606.03184).
2. Partial observability: belief-carrying predictors help in toy hidden-state tasks (arXiv:2605.25313); no real-data evidence; JEPA with irregular sampling is untested.
3. Dynamics: action-conditioning on observational data is fragile (teacher forcing makes actions inert, arXiv:2605.25313; identifiability needs diverse exogenous actions, arXiv:2609.31161; learned world models fail off-support in an economics benchmark, arXiv:2607.03144).
4. Use: the cheapest honest uses are nowcasting/interpolation and regime representation (Koopman view arXiv:2511.09783, HEPA VIX event result arXiv:2605.11130); scenario generation needs a stochastic head (arXiv:2607.05238, arXiv:2601.14354) not yet validated; counterfactual claims should be limited to synthetic known-mechanism tests (DSGE-Gym, FinStressTS) until identification is argued.

## 12. Addendum: items found after the first draft (arXiv API recovered; WebSearch)
Read depth as above. Notes: `literature/notes/arXiv-<id>.md`.
**More negative/neutral evidence on JEPA for time series (strengthens section 2, M-H).**
- Cross-machine CNC world model: latent-predictive pretraining gave **no in-domain forecasting gain** over matched from-scratch training (0.811 vs 0.813 RMSE, p=0.76) and trailed an RSSM (0.759) and an MLP (0.771) on source validation (arXiv:2609.16071 [FT]). With partial sensor overlap (17 vs 10 shared channels) zero-shot transfer beat persistence but trailed PatchTST/iTransformer; input normalisation (RevIN) alone closed the gap. So three independent evaluations (arXiv:2609.31680, arXiv:2609.25541 [A], arXiv:2609.16071) fail to show a reliable forecasting benefit from latent prediction on small/industrial/tabular data.
- Collapse can be invisible: a Graph-JEPA had healthy linear-probe accuracy (0.871) and effective rank (18-47/128) while retrieval recovered 0.00 of 14.4 bits; "category-conditional collapse" from the predictor+EMA objective (arXiv:2608.20516 [FT]). Implication for pre-registration: diagnostics must include instance-level retrieval and parameter-free controls, not just probe accuracy/effective rank.
- Other JEPA-for-time-series items with mostly positive self-reported results: SC-JEPA (anomaly prediction, soft codebook bottleneck needed, ablation without it "collapses to near-random"; 5 benchmarks vs 16 baselines; arXiv:2602.04643 [FT]); LeNEPA (next-latent prediction with temporal SIGReg, no EMA; UCR-128 mean RF accuracy 77.65%, within 0.24-1.16 points of MOMENT/Mantis, beats NuTime; single seed; assumes regular sampling; arXiv:2607.00958 [FT]); hierarchical JEPA on ECG (arXiv:2607.01145, per scan abstract); RD-JEPA few-trajectory transfer (pretrain on five reaction-diffusion systems, adapt with 1/5/10 trajectories; arXiv:2609.29403 [A]); flow-guided neural-operator SSL where corruption level is a learned degree of freedom (arXiv:2602.12267 [A]).
**Generation from JEPA, upgraded (section 5).**
- **SPLICE (arXiv:2605.00126 [FT])**: JEPA 64-d latent tokens + latent flow-matching/diffusion bridge + decoder + Adaptive Conformal Inference. [spot-check 2026-10-01: abstract gives lowest mean Load-only MSE 0.056, 9/12 non-degenerate datasets at 91-day gaps, 18/32 across gap lengths, best CRPS 0.161 (-18.3% vs strongest competitor), ACI 93-95% coverage, 13 load datasets of which 9 proprietary; the extraction's '25.1% vs VAE' and 'CRPS 0.182 vs 0.197' may come from an ablation table and are unverified: use abstract numbers.] Not evaluated on finance; strongly seasonal data; gaps shorter than a week or longer than three months uncharacterised. This is the closest existing template for "JEPA + sampling + calibrated uncertainty" and for the nowcasting/interpolation use (H on relevance, L on transfer to non-seasonal finance).
- **Likelihood from the JEPA itself:** the anti-collapse term provably estimates the data density; JEPA-SCORE = sum of log Jacobian singular values (arXiv:2510.05949 [FT]); shown on vision/Gaussian mixtures only, view-based (augmentation) setting. A decoder-free plausibility score for scenarios is therefore conceivable but unverified (speculation).
- **HMM view:** probabilistic JEPA has the filter/transition/emission structure of an HMM under stated conditions; MCJEPA with a transition matrix gives exact multi-horizon consistency (arXiv:2608.13621 [FT]); NextLat shows transformers trained with next-latent prediction provably converge to belief states (arXiv:2511.05963 [A]) and is a baseline in DSGE-Gym.
- Variational streaming flow adds stochasticity to a deterministic streaming-flow velocity field for probabilistic forecasting in physical time (arXiv:2610.00976, per scan abstract) and the "ClosurePairs" tests separate observation aliasing from intrinsic noise as sources of future branching, proving ordinary transitions cannot identify them (arXiv:2608.00591 [A]); the latter is directly relevant to deciding whether scenario spread is partial observability (reducible with more data) or genuine randomness.
- Generative JEPA in other domains (3D MRI Med-D-JEPA arXiv:2608.28787 [A]; JEPA-guided diffusion for minority sampling arXiv:2605.24631 [A]) shows the pattern "JEPA as semantic prior or latent token space, diffusion/flow for sampling" but is outside finance.
**Counterfactual and action-conditioning, upgraded (sections 1, 3, 7).**
- Latent state sufficiency: predictive sufficiency does not imply control sufficiency; passive prediction cannot identify counterfactual dynamics without action-conditioned data or causal structure; exact belief states subsume prediction and control (arXiv:2605.01694 [FT], conceptual).
- Do-JEPA: to separate what an action caused from what co-occurred, train on paired rollouts from saved simulator states (effect loss on latent differences); needs exact state restoration and a known reference action; gains of 28.4% latent effect RMSE on Push-T but 51-53% higher factual error from scratch (arXiv:2609.37378 [FT]). Counterfactual-collapse failure mode and a belief-state + action-conditioned latent remedy (CLWM, arXiv:2609.05834 [A]); Causal-JEPA object-level latent masking as structured partial observability (arXiv:2602.11389 [A]); decision-aligned latent world model because prediction accuracy does not guarantee that latent distance ranks executable candidates (arXiv:2609.24749 [A]); contrastive inverse-dynamics as anti-collapse pressure without a Gaussian prior (arXiv:2608.17542 [A]); subspace Gaussian regularisation as a softer SIGReg (arXiv:2605.09241 [A]).
- What a latent encodes depends on prediction targets, and probe readability can diverge from usage in forecasts (arXiv:2607.27017 [FT]); put the quantities you want to reason about (fundamentals) in the *target* views.
- Consolidated verdict (M-H): from observational financial data, an action-conditioned JEPA can at best learn conditional-on-action *predictions*; interpreting them as counterfactuals needs (i) an identification argument (arXiv:2609.31161 conditions), (ii) paired-intervention supervision, available only from simulators (arXiv:2609.37378, DSGE-Gym arXiv:2607.03144), and (iii) off-support tests, where learned models fail by up to ~40x (arXiv:2607.03144, per abstract).
**Graph / relational JEPAs (supply-chain wedge).** NodeJEPA (arXiv:2608.04381), HP-JEPA (arXiv:2608.00491), World-as-Graph (arXiv:2609.38927), Graph-JEPA (arXiv:2309.16014) are all [A]; none financial. Orthogonal predictive factorisation (arXiv:2608.20065, arXiv:2609.20800 [A]) decomposes latent targets so dominant signals do not starve weaker ones, a plausible fix for finance where a few dominant factors (market, rates) swamp idiosyncratic structure (speculation).
