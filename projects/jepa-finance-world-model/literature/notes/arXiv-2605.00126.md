# arXiv:2605.00126 SPLICE: latent diffusion over JEPA embeddings for conformal time-series inpainting
Read: HTML full text via WebFetch extraction (2026-10-01). Found by WebSearch, not in the early scan.
- **Method:** four stages: JEPA encoder maps daily segments to 64-d latent tokens; conditional latent bridge (flow matching / diffusion) generates candidate trajectories; hourly-conditioned decoder back to signal space; Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) wraps coverage bands.
- **Key ablation claim:** "fidelity is governed primarily by the predictive representation rather than by generative complexity": JEPA latent beats VAE encoder by 25.1% MSE and beats contrastive TS2Vec; 5-step flow-matching sampler matches/improves 50-step DDIM (5-10x faster).
- **Data:** 13 hourly electricity datasets (9 proprietary utility feeds NE-US 2015-2025, 3 UCI clients, ETTh1) + 12 CAMELS-SPAT hydrology catchments; 1,071-4,590 contiguous days each. No finance.
- **Baselines (7):** seasonal naive, BRITS, SAITS, CSDI, TimesNet, PatchTST, ImputeFormer. Lowest mean MSE 0.056, wins 9/12 non-degenerate electricity sets; CRPS 0.182 (-7.8% vs SAITS 0.197). Static CQR under-covers by up to 7.5 pts under non-stationarity; ACI holds 93-95% coverage.
- **Limitations (authors):** gaps shorter than 1 week or longer than 3 months uncharacterised; ACI = marginal, time-averaged coverage; covariate uncertainty not modelled; degrades on zero-inflated/bimodal loads (64-d latent loses fine structure).
- **Relevance (high):** the only paper found that combines JEPA latents + sampled generation + calibrated uncertainty, for missing-data filling (nowcasting/interpolation use). Exactly the "JEPA latent + generative head + decoder" architecture needed for scenarios, with an honest ablation that the representation matters more than the sampler. Not tested on non-stationary financial data; electricity is strongly seasonal, finance is not (H on the gap).
