# arXiv:2510.05949 Gaussian Embeddings: How JEPAs Secretly Learn Your Data Density
Read: HTML full text via WebFetch extraction (2026-10-01).
- **Theorem 1:** at optimality the JEPA anti-collapse term makes the embedding estimate the data density; sample probability p(x) proportional to the inverse of the expected product of Jacobian singular values; JEPA-SCORE(x) = sum_k log sigma_k(J_f(x)) (8 lines of PyTorch). Assumes Gaussian embeddings N(0, I/K), views generated by a transformation density, factorised p_X = p_mu x p_T; applies to VICReg, SimCLR, MoCo, CLIP, DINO, I-JEPA, DINOv2.
- **Evidence:** Gaussian mixtures; qualitative ImageNet-1k scores on DINOv2/I-JEPA/MetaCLIP; MNIST/Galaxy images score lower under ImageNet models (OOD detection). No limitations section; no non-vision test, no comparison to classical density estimators.
- **Relevance:** if it holds for time-series JEPAs it gives a sampling-free *likelihood proxy* for scenario plausibility and outlier/regime-shift detection without a decoder (speculation: untested outside vision, view-generation assumptions unlikely to match windowed financial series). Note it assumes the augmentation-view setting, not context-to-future prediction.
