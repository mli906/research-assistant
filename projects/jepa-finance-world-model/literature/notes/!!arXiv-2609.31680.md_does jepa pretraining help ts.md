# arXiv:2609.31680 Does JEPA Pretraining Help Time Series Forecasting? (Feng, Liao, Ng, Liang)
Read: HTML full text via WebFetch extraction (tool summarises; numbers below copied from that extraction, not re-verified in PDF). Accessed 2026-10-01.
- **Problem:** is JEPA pretraining a generally useful add-on for forecasting backbones?
- **Method:** one JEPA instantiation = LeJEPA (prediction L2 in embedding space + SIGReg, lambda=0.1, 64 slices). No EMA/teacher; single shared encoder; context view = input window, target view = forecast horizon (no masking). Pretrain then fine-tune vs supervised-only, epochs matched, same hyperparameters.
- **Data:** 11 benchmarks: ETTh1/h2/m1/m2, Weather (temporal); METR-LA, PEMS-BAY, PEMS03/04/07/08 (spatio-temporal). 9 backbones: Crossformer, iTransformer, Autoformer, FEDformer, PatchTST, ASTGCN, STAEformer, STTN, STGCN. No financial data.
- **Results:** large sign-flipping variance. Gains: FEDformer/Weather +7.9% RMSE, STGCN/PEMS-BAY +7.2% RMSE, STTN/PEMS07 +12.7% RMSE. Losses: Autoformer/Weather -12.0% RMSE, ASTGCN/PEMS08 -7.8% RMSE; iTransformer negative everywhere (-0.1 to -2.9%). Pattern: dense global-attention backbones gain little or lose; structurally constrained backbones (graphs, frequency decomposition, segment attention) gain.
- **Authors' limitations:** mechanism not identified; need to ablate predictor and SIGReg separately; one JEPA instantiation only.
- **Critique:** (i) no comparison to reconstruction SSL or to simply longer supervised training; (ii) no seeds/CIs reported in extraction; (iii) "JEPA" here is LeJEPA with window-to-horizon views, i.e. close to multi-horizon feature prediction; the finding may not transfer to EMA/masked variants. (iv) Important for us: the headline "JEPA helps" is not backbone-agnostic, so backbone choice is a design variable, not a nuisance.
