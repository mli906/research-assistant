# MACROCAST: vintage-consistent TSFM for real-time macro forecasting (arXiv:2606.28670)
Carriero, Pettenuzzo, Shekhar, 2026. Read: arXiv HTML full text via a summarising fetch tool (2026-10-01); numbers below are as returned, not re-checked against the PDF.

- **Problem:** pretrained TSFMs leak in two ways in macro forecasting: (1) temporal contamination (model has seen realised values of the target period) and (2) revision bias (trained on fully revised data, but a real-time forecaster only sees preliminary vintages).
- **Method:** 1M-parameter linear-RNN (GatedDeltaProduct), 9 quantile outputs. Pretrained on ~10M purely synthetic series (7 generator families, ~1 A6000 GPU-day), then fine-tuned per vintage on synthetic panels drawn from BVAR/DFM/AR(2)/block-bootstrap estimated on ALFRED vintage data (2,100 panels per vintage, ~9 min).
- **Eval:** 123 FRED-MD series, h=0..12 months, Aug 1999-Dec 2024 (Q1-Q2 2020 excluded), monthly real-time vintages from ALFRED, expanding windows, "6-month delayed actuals". Metric: RMSFE ratio vs AR(1). Baselines: AR(1), BVAR (Minnesota), DFM, Chronos-2 (120M, zero-shot), Moirai2-Small (zero-shot).
- **Results:** pooled RMSFE ratio 0.971 at h=1, 0.995 at h=12; beats AR(1) on ~80% of series-horizon pairs (86% at h=1). PAYEMS 0.747 at h=1; FEDFUNDS 0.863 at h=1; INDPRO ~0.99-1.00 (no real gain).
- **Critique / use for us:** (a) closest published template for a leakage-free macro evaluation protocol (vintage-by-vintage, delayed actuals); (b) gains are small outside labour-market series, i.e. the honest ceiling for macro nowcasting is low; (c) it never trains on real macro history, which sidesteps scarcity but means it learns BVAR/DFM dynamics, not "the economy"; (d) no CIs on ratios, FRED-MD only; (e) not a JEPA and not action-conditioned. Confidence in details: medium (summarised fetch).
