# Dynamic Agent Skills: lifecycle survey (arXiv:2607.10113)
Read: full text, partial (arXiv HTML via WebFetch extraction, 2026-09-25; long document, extraction summarised sections 5, 11 and 12). Tags: skills, core.

- **Scope.** 124-paper audit set, 2023–2026. Skills are framed as "lifecycle-managed, verified, evolving artifact stores".
- **Taxonomy (8 stages).** Evidence acquisition → proposal → verification and admission → organisation → retrieval and composition → maintenance and repair → distillation and portability → governance and provenance (lineage, authorship, safety checks, rollback).
- **Cross-user sharing.** The survey names a lifecycle family "Cross-user and registry-sharing systems" (SkillClaw, AutoSkill, SkillFlow, SkillsVote). Its assurance bottleneck is "ownership, privacy, retrieval quality, compatibility".
- **Safety surfaces (§11.1).** These include "ownership, confidentiality, and skill theft", "credential and secret leakage" and "attribution loss". §11.2 reports a "coverage gap across the surveyed literature". Only about 13 of the 124 papers form a governance/safety family.
- **Open problems (§12).** Portability, parametric collapse under repeated distillation, retrieval at scale, governance/provenance/attribution (12.7), benchmarks honest about dynamics.
- **Critique.** This is independent confirmation that governance of promoted skills is a recognised gap. Note that the survey frames privacy as **ownership/confidentiality of the skill** (IP), not **personal data of the users whose traces made it**. Erasure of source data is not among its stated open problems. That framing is the space the PI would occupy.
