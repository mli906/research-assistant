# AuthMem-Bench: authority collapse at the memory-consolidation boundary (arXiv:2608.01679, 2026-08-03)
Read: full text (arXiv HTML via curl + text extraction), 2026-09-25, by gap-analyst. Tags: pi-input, gaps-check.

- **Problem.** Consolidation turns role-labelled histories into durable items that are later used as a user fact, an attested observation or a standing instruction. "Authority collapse": the claim is kept but the source constraints on its use are erased (e.g. a third-party report becomes "The user lives in Seattle").
- **Design.** Paired benchmark: the focal claim and the downstream task are held fixed and only source authority varies. 50 frozen source histories, 7 pre-registered source-to-use transitions. Sources are τ-bench (airline, retail, telecom) and APIGen-MT-5k (airline, retail); operative values are synthetic. Modules: A (write-time collapse across 7 consolidators modelled on Mem0-style prompts × 7 backbones), B (controlled action-grounded evaluation), C (end-to-end with a predicted authority label).
- **Results.** Collapse in 48/49 configurations. Collapsed memories without authority metadata give a mean unauthorised-action rate of 50.3% (Module B). End to end: ASR 16.9% → 0.0% with predicted labels; TSR 39.7% → 40.0%.
- **Stated limitations / caveats (full text).** "metadata governs how retained information is used and cannot recover information omitted during consolidation"; "End-to-end utility remains recall-limited": the consolidator retained the focal proposition in only 139 of 350 cases. Action evaluation is tool-call-grounded, not environment-state-grounded (histories cannot be replayed). APIGen-MT-5k is CC BY-NC 4.0.
- **Scope (our reading).** Single principal (one user per history); authority = who asserted (user / assistant / tool / third party). No multi-principal scope (client, barrier, purpose), no retention obligations, no finance domain.
- **Bearing on gaps.** Pre-empts the generic "epistemic status of memories" candidate and most of G8's source-authority half. Leaves open: scope/barrier continuity in multi-client stores; finance authority lattices; and, via the 139/350 figure, the *retain-side* failure of consolidation (a mandated fact silently dropped), which feeds gap GP1.
