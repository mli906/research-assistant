---
name: research-status
description: Summarize where a research project stands and what's next.
argument-hint: [project-slug | "all"]
---
Arguments: $ARGUMENTS.

Read `STATE.md`, `experiments/log.md` (last entries), and list which artifacts exist.
Report: current stage, last decisions, open questions waiting on the PI, running
jobs (check for background processes or recent log output), and the recommended next
command. For "all", give one line per project in `projects/`.
